## What are Computer-Use Agents (CUA)?
- AI agents operating on computer
	- Automated Task completion via GUIs, CLIs or APIs
	- Can be reactive or proatctive
- Related concepts
	- Browser-use agents: limited to only tasks within a browser

## Why CUAs?
- Saving human efforts
	- Automation
- Accessibility
- Scale and Capability
	- Speed and scale impossible for a human
- Assistance and Collaboration
	- Simplifying complex workflows and reducing cognitive load

## CUA Types
- Proactive vs reactive
- Reactive: triggered by user request, executed on demand 
- Proactive: anticipates user request
